THE CRAFT

How to run the chair

Read the request, choose the right tool, and finish before patience runs out.

Guard it

Clippers cut toward the selected guard. Use skin at the neckline and longer guards higher up for a fade.

Shape it

Scissors remove small amounts. Use them for the top length, texture, and fine beard detail.

Shave it

The razor only shaves facial hair. Glide over the jaw, chin, and mustache for a clean finish.

Color it

Pick a dye swatch and mist evenly across the scalp. Check the request for the exact color.

Refresh it

Wash & Dry raises comfort and mood. A relaxed client tips better and waits longer.

Turn it

Spin the chair to reach the back and sides. Finish with the gold button when the look is clean.
